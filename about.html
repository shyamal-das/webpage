About Me

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar, where I work in the area of protection and control of inverter-interfaced microgrids. My research focuses on developing reliable protection techniques for inverter-dominated power systems, with particular emphasis on islanding detection, grid-forming inverter dynamics, and protection of renewable-rich distribution networks.

My research combines analytical modelling, control system design, numerical simulation, and hardware validation using Power Hardware-in-the-Loop (PHIL), RTDS, and Typhoon HIL platforms. Through this work, I aim to bridge the gap between theoretical developments and practical implementation of modern power system protection schemes.

I am particularly interested in developing practical solutions that improve the reliability, resilience, and stability of future power systems with high penetration of inverter-based renewable energy resources.
