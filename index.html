Microgrid Protection • Grid-Forming Inverters • Renewable Integration • Power System Protection

I am a PhD Research Scholar in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bhubaneswar. My research focuses on protection and control of inverter-interfaced microgrids, with particular emphasis on grid-forming inverters, islanding detection, and inverter dynamics. My work combines analytical modelling with hardware validation using PHIL, RTDS, and Typhoon HIL, ensuring that the proposed methods are verified beyond simulation.
