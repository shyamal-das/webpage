Power & Energy Systems

Shyamal Das

I'm a PhD Research Scholar in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bhubaneswar, working on protection and control of inverter-dominated microgrids. My research spans islanding detection, grid-forming and grid-following inverter dynamics, and coordinated protection schemes for DER-rich systems — validated on real hardware through PHIL testing, RTDS, and Typhoon HIL, not only in simulation.
